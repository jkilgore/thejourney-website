What is The Journey?

More than a retreat. A community.

We believe every high school student deserves a space to ask big questions, find real answers, and discover the love God has for them.

🙏

Rooted in Faith

Each retreat weekend features 11 talks from youth, adults, and clergy — all centered on the love, mercy, and grace God has for every person in that room.

🤝

Built on Community

The bonds formed on a Journey retreat last far beyond the weekend. Students leave with new friendships and a supportive community.

✨

Space to Grow

Between the laughing and the learning, the praying and the participating, something transformational happens. Students are connected to a God who loves them.

🎉

Actually Fun

A Journey weekend is anything but boring. Expect games, music, great food, and the kind of late-night conversations you still think about years later.

Our Vision

Guiding students into a deep relationship with Christ to develop Godly character.
