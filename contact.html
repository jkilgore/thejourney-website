Quick answers

Common questions

How do I apply for a retreat?

Visit our Application page and fill out the form. Applications are open before each retreat weekend.

Who can attend?

High school students ages 14–19 from Galesburg and surrounding communities. All faith backgrounds welcome.

How can I volunteer?

We'd love to have you. Email us or reach out on Facebook and we'll get you connected.

How can I donate?

Visit our Donate page for information on how to give and support the ministry financially.
