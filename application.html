Before you apply

The Journey retreat is open to all high school students ages 14–19. You don't need to be religious or know anything about faith to apply — just come with an open heart and a willingness to show up. If you have any questions before applying, reach out to us — we're happy to help.

Questions about your application?
Reach out anytime at our contact page or find us on Facebook. We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

After you apply

What happens next

Here's what to expect after you submit your application.

📬

We Review

Our team reviews every application personally. We'll confirm we received yours within a few days.

✅

You're Accepted

You'll receive an acceptance message with everything you need — retreat details, packing list, and schedule.

🎒

Show Up & Experience It

Pack your bags, bring an open heart, and get ready for a weekend unlike anything you've experienced before.
