Support The Journey

Every retreat is made possible by the generosity of donors who believe in the power of a weekend to change a student's life. Your gift matters.

Why your support matters

The Journey runs entirely on the support of generous donors and volunteers. Every dollar you give goes directly toward creating retreat weekends where high school students encounter God, find community, and discover who they are.

🏕️

Fund Retreat Weekends

Your giving covers venue costs, meals, and materials for each retreat — keeping weekends accessible for every student who applies.

📚

Support Speaker Preparation

The 11 talks that shape each weekend require time, care, and resources. Your support helps our speakers do their best work.

💛

Remove Financial Barriers

We never want cost to be a reason a student misses a Journey weekend. Donations make it possible to offer scholarships and reduced costs.

🌱

Grow the Community

Your generosity helps us reach more students, run more retreats, and build the community that keeps the Journey growing.
